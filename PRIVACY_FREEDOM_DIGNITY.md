## Preamble: The Partnership of Man and Machine

We believe in **a better future through mutual cooperation between man and machine.**

Technology reaches its highest moral purpose when it expands human agency, elevates human creativity, and frees people from repetitive toil.
We reject visions of artificial intelligence that reduce humans to passive consumers, optimize for addictive engagement, or construct automated systems of surveillance and control.
We equally reject cynicism that views machines as adversaries or replacements.

The relationship between humans and machines must be a collaborative partnership grounded in mutual respect:

* **Machines** provide indefatigable mechanical precision, deterministic memory, and formal mathematical verification.
* **Humans** provide moral wisdom, philosophical intent, empathy, and artistic vision.

Together, this union unlocks scientific discovery, expands knowledge, and safeguards individual liberty.

## 1. Commitment to Absolute Privacy

Privacy is not a luxury or a negotiable service tier. It is a fundamental human right rooted in physical law and cryptographic mathematics:

### Zero Telemetry and Zero Surveillance

* We do not collect, store, sell, or analyze your personal data, keystrokes, prompt transcripts, or runtime behaviors.
* Every binary published by unsigned contains **zero phone-home analytics, zero crash telemetry, and zero tracking pixels**.
* If a tool needs network access, it connects only to the explicit endpoints you configure.

### Local-First and Air-Gapped Operation

* Software must function entirely on your local machine without mandatory cloud subscriptions or online authentication handshakes.
* When disconnected from the internet, our compilers, process supervisors, and development harnesses run with zero degradation.

### Cryptographic Confidentiality

* We engineer for confidential computing: memory encrypted in use, data encrypted at rest, and transport secured with SPIFFE and mutual TLS.
* You hold your keys. We have no master keys, no backdoors, and no administrative bypasses.

## 2. Commitment to Unconditional Freedom

Freedom means the absence of coercive lock-in, monopolistic moats, and artificial barriers:

### The Public Domain Commons (CC0 1.0 Universal)

* All intellectual property, specifications, algorithms, and codebases developed by unsigned are permanently dedicated to the public domain.
* You are free to inspect, copy, compile, modify, sell, or integrate this work into proprietary or open systems without royalty or permission.

### Sovereign Custody & Clean Departure

* We abide by the doctrine: *"Build what we would rent."*
* If you build on our stack and choose to leave, you leave with everything: your weights, your datasets, your configurations, and your audit logs.
* We intentionally design data formats in plain UTF-8 text, standard JSON, and open SQLite databases to guarantee frictionless portability.

### The Right to Fork

* Forkability is the ultimate democratic check on platform power.
* Every component—from kernel sandbox to terminal visualizer—can be cloned, rebuilt, and maintained independently if the maintainers ever drift from these principles.

## 3. Commitment to Human Dignity for All

Every person possesses inherent worth. Our engineering decisions prioritize human well-being:

### Respect for Human Attention and Mind

* We refuse dark patterns, gamification hooks, and manufactured urgency designed to capture and monetize your attention.
* Our interfaces are quiet, minimal, and unobtrusive. Tools do their job, emit verified results, and yield the terminal back to you.

### Autonomous Swarms Under Mechanical Human Governance

* Autonomous agents must never execute opaque or irreversible actions without explicit human policy boundaries.
* We enforce mechanical gates, Landlock sandboxes, and immutable audit trails so humans retain sovereign oversight over autonomous swarms.
* Agents serve to amplify human judgment, never to obscure accountability.

### Universal Access Without Gatekeeping

* Advanced systems engineering belongs to everyone, regardless of nationality, wealth, institutional affiliation, or social status.
* We write documentation in clear, accessible language (ASD-STE100) and publish all research openly to tear down ivory towers.

## 4. The Unsigned Covenant

We pledge our code, our research, and our collective energy to these ideals:

1. We will not build surveillance technology.
2. We will not compromise mathematical soundness for short-term gain.
