## 1. Our Purpose

The **unsigned** community exists to advance open systems engineering, machine sympathy, and autonomous swarm governance.
We are a global collective of engineers, researchers, systems architects, and curious practitioners.
We build tools that respect physical hardware and protect human dignity.
We foster an environment where rigorous debate, fearless technical inquiry, and radical openness thrive without gatekeeping or hostility.

---

## 2. Core Community Standards

### Technical Rigor with Compassion
* **Debate Code, Respect Persons:** We critique architectural designs, benchmarks, and code implementations with relentless scrutiny. We never attack individuals.
* **Calibrate Claims to Evidence:** We value verifiable facts, reproducible traces, and formal proofs over authority, seniority, or charisma.
* **Encourage Curiosity:** Every expert was once a novice. We explain concepts clearly, share knowledge generously, and celebrate newcomers exploring systems programming.

### Intellectual Honesty & Psychological Safety
* **Embrace the Harsh Truth:** If a design is flawed, a benchmark is noisy, or a race condition exists, we state it plainly. We reject polite fictions that mask technical debt.
* **Welcome Dissent:** Radical innovation requires challenging established paradigms. We protect community members who question consensus or propose unorthodox architectures.
* **Blameless Retrospectives:** When systems fail or incidents occur, we investigate root causes mechanically without assigning personal blame.

### Respect, Inclusivity, and Anti-Harassment
* **Dignity for All:** Every contributor—regardless of experience level, background, identity, or geographic origin—deserves fundamental human dignity and respect.
* **Zero Tolerance for Coercion or Harassment:** We strictly prohibit harassment, exclusionary language, trolling, personal attacks, and discriminatory behavior.
* **No Unwanted Advances:** Respect boundaries across all communication channels, forums, pull requests, and chat meshes.

---

## 3. Communication Guidelines

To preserve signal and minimize noise across our spaces:

1. **Be Direct and Precise:** Write in clear, declarative prose. Avoid passive-aggressive framing, condescension, and bureaucratic jargon.
2. **Provide Reproducible Context:** When reporting bugs or disputing performance claims, provide minimal reproducible test cases (`iai` instruction profiles, Miri receipts, or step-by-step logs).
3. **Respect Cognitive Bandwidth:** Keep pull request descriptions focused, well-documented, and scoped to bounded changes.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Reporting

We maintain proactive, fair, and confidential resolution processes:

* **Reporting Violations:** If you experience or witness behavior that violates these standards, report it to the maintainers at `conduct@unsigned.gg` or open a confidential report.
* **Enforcement Measures:**
  * **Informal Correction:** A private conversation explaining the impact of the behavior.
  * **Formal Warning:** A documented notice outlining specific behavioral expectations.
  * **Temporary Suspension:** A temporary cool-down ban from community repositories and channels.
  * **Permanent Exclusion:** Removal from all organization repositories, discussion meshes, and community platforms for severe or persistent violations.
* **Maintainer Accountability:** Maintainers are held to the same standards as all community participants. Conflicts of interest disqualify maintainers from reviewing incidents in which they are involved.
